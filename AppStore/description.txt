Source Shelf keeps research sources, exact page or section locators, quote cards, and personal notes on your device. Enter source details yourself; organize sources with tags and collections. Search titles and export a portable JSON backup, Markdown cards, or BibTeX and APA drafts for human proofreading. Import backups with a choice to merge or replace your local library. No account, cloud sync, trackers, or automatic metadata lookup.

Files and copied citations may include private notes and verbatim quotes. Review before sharing. Source Shelf is under development; Windows portable release and mobile store distribution are not yet available.
