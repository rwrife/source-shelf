Source Shelf is a planned offline source-and-quote cabinet for reading research on phone, tablet, and Windows. Its planned workflow lets you enter book, article, document, and interview metadata; save exact page or section locators with quote cards and personal notes; organize sources by tags and collections; and export portable JSON and citation drafts for review. Your notes stay on your device. No account or cloud sync is required.

This repository is under development. These features describe intended behavior, not a currently released app. Citation output will require human proofreading before publication.
